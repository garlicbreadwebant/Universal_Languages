# Esperanto collection changelog

## 0.2.0 — October 18, 2026

- Expanded from 219 to 397 English lookup entries, covering every original Lumena and Narela headword plus supporting vocabulary.
- Preserved English lookup variants and distinguished grammatical constructions, word senses, and conventional phrases.
- Added shared-vocabulary validation to keep all three dictionaries and their website training data aligned.

## 0.1 — October 18, 2026

- Added a starter lexicon and generated English–Esperanto and Esperanto–English dictionaries.
- Added overview, pronunciation, grammar, lessons, and editorial/source notes in the same structure as Lumena and Narela.
- Integrated the collection into the shared website, offline practice, and publication checks.

This version identifies the project's learning collection, not a version of the Esperanto language.

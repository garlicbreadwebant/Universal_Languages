# Esperanto pronunciation

## Alphabet

Esperanto has 28 letters: a b c ĉ d e f g ĝ h ĥ i j ĵ k l m n o p r s ŝ t u ŭ v z. Q, w, x, and y are not part of the Esperanto alphabet. Each letter has a consistent sound; English comparisons below are approximate.

| Letter | Sound | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a | /a/ as in father | patro — father |
| e | /e/ a pure vowel without an English-style glide | bela — beautiful |
| i | /i/ as in machine | mi — I |
| o | /o/ a pure vowel without a glide | domo — house |
| u | /u/ as in flute | unu — one |
| c | /ts/ as in cats | celo — goal |
| ĉ | /tʃ/ as in church | ĉambro — room |
| g | /ɡ/ as in go | granda — big |
| ĝ | /dʒ/ as in gem | ĝardeno — garden |
| h | /h/ as in hat | hundo — dog |
| ĥ | /x/ as in Scottish loch | ĥoro — choir |
| j | /j/ as in yes | jes — yes |
| ĵ | /ʒ/ as in vision | ĵurnalo — newspaper |
| r | tapped or trilled /r/ | ruĝa — red |
| s | /s/ as in sun | salo — salt |
| ŝ | /ʃ/ as in ship | ŝi — she |
| ŭ | /w/ a nonsyllabic glide | aŭ — or |
| z | /z/ as in zoo | zorge — carefully |

B, d, f, k, l, m, n, p, t, and v are broadly similar to their usual English consonant sounds. Pronounce all consonants in clusters; do not silently drop final letters.

## Stress and syllables

Stress the next-to-last syllable: sa-LU-ton, es-pe-RAN-to, ler-NA-do. Each a, e, i, o, or u makes a syllable. J and ŭ do not: one-syllable aŭ contrasts with two-syllable au. In a word such as familio, pronounce the separate vowels: fa-mi-LI-o.

## Typing and listening

Use Unicode ĉ, ĝ, ĥ, ĵ, ŝ, and ŭ in this collection. The common x-system writes cx, gx, hx, jx, sx, and ux when needed for input; convert it before editing the CSV. The website's accent-insensitive search accepts gardeno for ĝardeno, but does not convert x-system input.

Browser and AI voices may approximate Esperanto incorrectly. Compare their output with the written rules.

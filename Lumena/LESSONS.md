# First lessons

Spend as much time as needed on each lesson. These are practice units, not a promise of fluency within a fixed number of days. Sound out the words using the pronunciation guide.

## 1. Meet someone

| Lumena | English |
|---|---|
| sali. | Hello. |
| nami te mi esa Ana. | My name is Ana. |
| nami te ti esa ke? | What is your name? |
| mi esa penu. | I am a person. |
| sani. | Three. |

Ana is an illustrative proper name. Names retain their owners' spelling. Practice `mi`, `ti`, and `li` without changing the verb `esa`.

## 2. Ask for help

| Lumena | English |
|---|---|
| pali sama mi. | Please help me. |
| mi no kowa. | I do not understand. |
| pali tala me senu. | Please speak slowly. |
| aku esa kana? | Where is the water? |
| mi neta nupa ni aku. | I need food and water. |
| tani. | Thank you. |
| yali. | You are welcome. |

## 3. Describe an action

| Lumena | English |
|---|---|
| mi lena Lumena. | I learn Lumena. |
| mi tu lena Lumena. | I am learning Lumena. |
| mu pa pala a tama pati. | We walked home yesterday. |
| lu pu wena puti. | They will come tomorrow. |
| ti wola ta puka ka? | Do you want this book? |
| nila sewa tuli wiki. | A child sees two birds. |

`tama` means a dwelling; `a tama` is “to a dwelling/home,” with the specific home supplied by context. Add `te mi` to specify mine.

## 4. Explain and connect

| Lumena | English |
|---|---|
| mi pote leta. | I can read. |
| mi ama ti. | I love you. |
| si ti wena sipu mi pu pami nupa. | If you come, I will make food. |
| mi esa yomi pako ti esa na ta tama. | I am happy because you are in this house. |
| mi sawa pasi ti pa wena pasu. | I know that you came. |
| penu yasi ye sama mi yasu esa na tama. | The person who helps me is in the house. |

## 5. Mini-dialogue

| Speaker | Lumena | English |
|---|---|---|
| A | sali. ti tala Lumena ka? | Hello. Do you speak Lumena? |
| B | mi tu lena Lumena. pali tala me senu. | I am learning Lumena. Please speak slowly. |
| A | ti neta ke? | What do you need? |
| B | mi neta aku. | I need water. |
| A | aku esa na ta tama. | Water is in this house. |
| B | tani. | Thank you. |

## Practice

Translate before looking at the answers:

1. I do not see you.
2. Will they come?
3. My two books.
4. Who helps you?
5. I know that you are learning.
6. Twenty-three people.
7. I cannot walk.
8. Please do not go.

Answers:

1. `mi no sewa ti.`
2. `lu pu wena ka?`
3. `tuli puka te mi`
4. `ki sama ti?`
5. `mi sawa pasi ti tu lena pasu.`
6. `tuli teka sani penu`
7. `mi no pote pala.`
8. `pali no wata!`

Practice repairing misunderstanding: say `mi no kowa`, ask `ke?`, repeat the relevant noun, and use a full clause. Reading these answers checks the rules; conversation with diverse learners is needed to check real communication.
